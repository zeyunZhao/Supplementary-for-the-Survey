# Supplementary Materials for Neurostimulation Survey

## Associated Publication

**Title:** A Survey of Recent Advances in Computational Models and Machine Learning Applications in Neurostimulation

**Journal:** IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics (JBHI)

**DOI:** 

## Overview

This repository provides the original manuscript, a reduced version, and supplementary materials associated with our survey on computational modeling (CM), machine learning (ML), and deep learning (DL) applications in neurostimulation.

The reduced version was prepared to address the journal's 14-page limit while preserving the main scientific contributions. Detailed discussions, additional figures, comparison tables, and methodological information removed from the main text have been retained in the supplementary document.

## File Descriptions

### 1. [Original.pdf](Original.pdf) — Original Manuscript

The full-length version of the manuscript before page reduction. It serves as a reference for comparing the original and revised content.

### 2. [Reduced_version.pdf](Reduced_version.pdf) — Reduced Manuscript

The shortened version prepared to meet the journal's page limit. Redundant descriptions and selected detailed materials have been removed or condensed while retaining the core methodology, findings, clinical implications, and conclusions.

### 3. [Supplementary.pdf](Supplementary.pdf) — Supplementary Materials

Contains extended content relocated from the main manuscript, including detailed literature search strategies, distributions of computational models and research tasks, methodological comparisons, and computational tools for neurostimulation modeling.

## Relationship Between the Files

**Original Manuscript → Reduced Manuscript + Supplementary Materials**

The reduced manuscript presents the essential scientific content, while the supplementary document preserves additional details for transparency, reproducibility, and further reference.

Together, these documents are intended to maintain the completeness of the original survey while satisfying the journal's publication requirements.

## Publication Status

The proposed reductions and supplementary materials are provided for editorial review and remain subject to approval by the Editor-in-Chief (EIC). The reduced manuscript should not be regarded as the final published version until approved.
